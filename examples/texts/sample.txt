Put any UTF-8 text here. A paragraph may continue across several lines because
ordinary line breaks are changed to spaces when the file is loaded.

A blank line starts a new paragraph. During a quick speed test, SerialTypist picks
whole paragraphs at random and combines enough of them to fill the test.

You may add more files and subdirectories. Only files whose names end in .txt
are loaded, so notes and unrelated files are safely ignored.
