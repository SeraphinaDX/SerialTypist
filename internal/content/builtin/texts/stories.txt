The little train crossed the valley just before sunset. Its windows glowed against the blue hills, and every turn of the track revealed another quiet farm. Inside, travelers read books, shared snacks, and watched the evening gather around them.

Mira found an old brass key beneath the garden gate. It did not fit the shed, the cellar, or the wooden box on the shelf. At last she tried the tiny door painted on the back of the greenhouse, and the door swung open.

Rain tapped a patient beat on the roof while Rowan repaired the radio. One wire was loose, then another, but soon a warm voice emerged from the static. The forecast promised clear skies and a bright weekend by the lake.
