Morning light reached across the desk while the kettle began to sing. A notebook waited beside the keyboard, open to a clean page. The day felt unhurried, full of small tasks that could be finished one at a time.

Good typing grows from accuracy and a relaxed rhythm. Keep your hands light, return your fingers to the home row, and look at the words ahead. Speed arrives naturally when correct movement becomes familiar.

A terminal can be a wonderfully focused place to work. There are no floating windows to chase and no unnecessary buttons to press. A clear prompt, a useful program, and a comfortable keyboard may be all that is needed.
